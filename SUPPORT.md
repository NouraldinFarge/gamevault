# Support

GameVault is an active portfolio project. Public support is focused on reproducible setup, portable-library, archive-review, metadata, accessibility, and release-verification questions.

## Choose the right channel

| Need | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Download, checksum, or first-run help | Read [Download and run](README.md#download-and-run), then open a [usage question](https://github.com/NouraldinFarge/gamevault/issues/new?template=question.yml) |
| Reproducible product defect | Open a [bug report](https://github.com/NouraldinFarge/gamevault/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| Focused product improvement | Open a [feature request](https://github.com/NouraldinFarge/gamevault/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) |
| Path traversal, unsafe extraction, allowlist bypass, command injection, or credential exposure | Use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/NouraldinFarge/gamevault/security/advisories/new) |

Do not upload game files, private archives, credentials, license keys, personal library databases, or screenshots/logs containing unredacted personal paths. Synthetic fixtures and procedural artwork are welcome.

## Product boundaries

GameVault does not provide game downloads, ownership bypass, DRM modification, antivirus guarantees, sandboxing, or recovery for commercial game files. It organizes and launches local, user-owned files and presents archive findings for explicit review.
